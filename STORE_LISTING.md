### Description (max 4000 chars)
```
Sunnah Umrah is a calm, step-by-step companion for performing ʿUmrah on the
Sunnah. Designed for first-timers and seasoned pilgrims alike — from the
moment you put on iḥrām to the final hair trim.

✦ STEP-BY-STEP TAWAF, SAʿI & HALQ
Lap-by-lap walkthrough of all 7 Tawaf rounds and all 7 Saʿi trips. Never
lose count. Never wonder "what do I say at this corner?" again.

✦ HAND-VETTED DU'AS — ARABIC + ENGLISH
Every du'a is sourced from authentic Salafi-aligned references — Ḥiṣn al-
Muslim, Ṣaḥīḥ Bukhārī & Muslim. Tap to listen to Arabic pronunciation.

✦ 26 PLACES TO VISIT (ZIYĀRAH)
Explore Jabal al-Nūr (Cave Hira), Mount Arafat, Cave Thawr, Masjid an-
Nabawī, the Rawḍah, Quba, Mount Uḥud, the Seven Mosques, the historic
Mīqāts and more — with photos, history, and one-tap "Open in Google Maps".

✦ GPS GATE-FINDER WHEN YOU'RE LOST
Lost in the Ḥaram crowds? One tap shows you the nearest of the 12 main
gates with a bearing-aware compass and walking distance.

✦ AI COMPANION FOR YOUR QUESTIONS
Powered by Claude AI. Ask any Fiqh question — what to do if you forget a
lap, whether women should jog during Tawaf, what to recite if you don't
know the du'a — calm, on-the-Sunnah answers.

✦ STAY TOGETHER WITH YOUR FAMILY
Create a private group. Share a QR code or one-tap invite link. Watch
each other's tawaf/saʿi count and approximate location update in real
time so you never lose a relative in the crowd.

✦ FULLY OFFLINE INSIDE THE ḤARAM
Photos, du'as, and instructions are pre-cached for the moments your
mobile signal drops out (which it will, inside the Ḥaram).

✦ HOTELS, ʿUMRAH PACKAGES & ESIM
Search hotels in Makkah and Madīnah, browse hand-picked ʿUmrah travel
agencies, and grab a Saudi-Arabia eSIM for instant data on arrival.

✦ ENGLISH + ARABIC, RIGHT-TO-LEFT
Every word — UI, du'as, and AI Companion — works flawlessly in both
languages. More languages coming soon.

May Allah accept your ʿUmrah — taqabbalAllāhu minnā wa minkum.

🤲 Built by a small team who care deeply about getting the Sunnah right.
If you spot anything we can improve, email us at hello@sunnahumrah.com.

✶ FREE — supported by optional Sadaqah and travel partners ✶
```
