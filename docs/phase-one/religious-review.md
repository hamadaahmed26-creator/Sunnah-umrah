# Religious content review for the isolated companion

Editorial check dated 20 November 2026. This register covers every English and Arabic stage, practice question, answer pair, displayed supplication and Help topic defined in `frontend/public/companion/content.js`. The material has been compared with the references below. A qualified human scholar has yet to approve the wording for worship use. No reviewer name or signature has been supplied.

## Instruction and quiz evidence

In the table, “Ibn Baz’s guide” refers to *Description of Umrah*, issued by his office, reproduced in *Collected Fatwas*, vol. 17, p. 425 onward, hosted by the Presidency of Religious Affairs at the Two Holy Mosques. The guide covers the normal sequence followed by this preview. [Arabic guide](https://risala.prh.gov.sa/gu/content-download?url=contents%2F63%2Far_sifat_omrah_ibnbaz.pdf)

| Card and quiz answer | Content and evidence checked |
| --- | --- |
| Prepare for ihram • before entering ihram | Bathing, the two male garments and modest women's garments follow Ibn Baz’s guide. The garment exceptions for women are niqab and gloves. ʿAʾishah describes perfuming the Prophet before ihram. Ibn ‘Uthaymin distinguishes the body from ihram clothes. Ibn Baz, vol. 10, p. 39, addresses a woman going out among non-mahram men with noticeable perfume. The Permanent Committee addresses fresh perfume for either sex after entry. [Bukhari 1539](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:1539) · [Bukhari 1838](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:1838) · [Nasa’i 5126](https://sunnah.com/nasai:5126) · [Ibn ‘Uthaymin, vol. 22, p. 9](https://islamqa.info/en/answers/194704) · [Ibn Baz](https://islamqa.info/en/answers/26347) · [Permanent Committee](https://islamqa.info/en/answers/109331) |
| Enter at the Miqat • prepare before the crossing | Ibn ‘Abbas reports the appointed boundaries for people arriving with pilgrimage intent. Ibn Baz’s guide supplies the inward intention and the Arabic words for Umrah. The air-travel reference applies crossing to flying alongside the boundary. The card specifies an incoming pilgrim and refers other itineraries to a guide. [Bukhari 1524](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:1524) · [Miqat on a plane](https://islamqa.info/en/answers/132269) |
| Talbiyah • stop as Tawaf begins | The displayed Arabic follows Ibn ‘Umar’s report. English gives the sense of the repeated response, praise, blessing, sovereignty and absence of partners. The transition into Tawaf follows Ibn Baz’s guide. [Bukhari 1549](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:1549) |
| Enter the mosque • ask for Allah’s mercy | Abu Humayd or Abu Usayd reports the entrance supplication. The Arabic is the entrance portion. Ibn Baz’s guide gives the right foot. Meeting arrangements and staff directions are practical suggestions. [Muslim 713a](https://sunnah.com/muslim:713a) |
| Tawaf • returning to the starting line completes a circuit | Ibn Baz gives ritual purity, waiting until menstruation ends and bathing, seven circuits, direction, avoiding crowding the Stone, freedom to supplicate, and the two rak‘ahs. Jabir describes the Prophet’s Tawaf and prayer. The Permanent Committee specifies passing outside the Hijr. The card introduces the guide’s position on purity and directs personal circumstances to scholars. [Muslim 1218a](https://sunnah.com/muslim:1218a) · [Permanent Committee on the Hijr](https://islamqa.info/en/answers/109275) |
| Sa‘i • an outward trip and return count as two | The Qur’an identifies Safa and Marwah. Jabir and Ibn Baz’s guide supply the sequence, orientation and remembrance. The last arrival is at Marwah. Ibn Baz recommends supplication there, while Ibn ‘Uthaymin describes supplication at the beginning of a trip. The quiz tests counting. The UI does not make a closing supplication a condition of completion. [Qur’an 2:158](https://quran.com/2/158) · [Muslim 1218a](https://sunnah.com/muslim:1218a) · [Documented positions including Ibn ‘Uthaymin, Al-Sharh al-Mumti‘ 7/352](https://islamqa.info/en/answers/617842) |
| Complete Umrah • shaving or trimming | Ibn Baz addresses shaving or shortening for men, trimming for women and leaving ihram after completing the rites. The supplementary discussion explains shortening all the hair, permits less than a fingertip and describes this as an approximate recommended amount. The card gives an actionable amount without requiring measurement. [Discussion citing Ibn Qudamah and the Permanent Committee](https://islamqa.info/en/answers/110804) |

## On-screen recitations in worship.js

For the Black Stone the text uses the takbir described by Ibn Abbas in Bukhari 1613. The dua between the two corners follows Abu Dawud 1892, also the supplication within Qur’an 2:201. Ibn Baz discusses both in Collected Fatwas 17/222, https://binbaz.org.sa/fatwas/15716 . The two rak‘ahs, initial approach to Safa, opening words of 2:158, and repeated remembrance at the hills follow Jabir in Muslim 1218a. The Arabic shown for the hilltop remembrance follows this narration specifically. The English sense and transliteration are editorial aids. On reaching Marwah for the seventh time the remembrance is optionally viewable and the pilgrim can proceed to hair cutting. The initial Safa words appear only at count zero. Publisher audio lessons are identified in human-recordings.md. Human phrase-by-phrase listening and scholarly sign-off remain outstanding.
