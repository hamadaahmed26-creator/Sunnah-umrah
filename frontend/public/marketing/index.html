"Is it free?"

Yes. Completely free, no ads, no tracking. You don't even need to sign in. We don't collect emails or personal data. May Allah accept your Umrah 🤲

"When Android?"

Soon insha'Allah — submitting to Google Play now. Tap the Android link in our bio to get an email when it goes live. (You can also use the full web app at sunnahumrah.app right now from any phone.)

"Can I use this for Hajj?"

The Tour is currently focused on ʿUmrah. A Hajj-specific Tour is on the roadmap insha'Allah. The Qibla, group, prayer times, FAQ and dua sections all work for Hajj already.

"Who built this?"

One brother, alone, in the UK. Built it for my own family first. Glad it's reaching yours too. JazakAllāhu khayran 🤲
